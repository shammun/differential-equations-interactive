TRIGONOMETRIC IDENTITY
+ help
a cos(ωt) + b sin(ωt) = A cos(ωt − φ)
  • All four sliders are independent. The magenta curve is A cos(ωt − φ); the green curve is a cos(ωt) + b sin(ωt). In general they are different curves.
  • The two checkboxes are display toggles for the plane on the right, and either or both may be ticked:
    a,b draws the point (a, b) with its horizontal leg a and vertical leg b;
    A,φ draws the arrow of length A at angle φ, with the circle of radius A.
  • Tick both and adjust the sliders until the point lands exactly on the arrow tip. At that moment A = √(a²+b²) and φ = atan2(b, a) — and the magenta and green curves coincide. That is the identity.
  • ω changes the frequency of everything at once and never affects A, φ, a or b.
