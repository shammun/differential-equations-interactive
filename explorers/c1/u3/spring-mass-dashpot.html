The mass at the moment of release

18.03 Recitation 6, Problem 2 — m = 1 kg, k = 17/16 N/m, b = 1/2 N·s/m

The mass is held 1 m to the right of equilibrium and shoved outward at 0.75 m/s, then let go. Press release to start the clock at that instant.

The bar at the far left is the mass’s own track, stood upright so that right in the apparatus becomes up on the page. The block slides along it and never sideways; the curve appears only because time carries the pen rightward across the page.

The four marked crossings are at 3π/4, 7π/4, 11π/4 and 15π/4 seconds — evenly spaced by π, which is half the pseudoperiod of 2π ≈ 6.28 s.
