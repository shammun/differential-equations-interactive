The graphing window at upper right displays solutions of the differential equation mẍ + bẋ + kx = Acos(ωt) or its associated homogeneous equation.

Three buttons at lower right toggle display of the steady state solution (in green), the solution with the given initial condition (in orange), and the corresponding transient (in blue). Rolling the cursor over that window creates crosshairs and a readout of t and x.

The time value is set using the slider under the window. The [>>] key starts an animation; while running it reads [||]. At the end of the run it reads [<<], which resets t to 0.

Sliders control the values of the parameters m, b, k, A and ω. The slider to the right of A sets the length of the time axis, from 0 to 100π.

At the upper left is a window displaying velocity against position. The state at t = 0 is controlled by dragging the orange diamond, or by grabbing the sliders along the left and lower edges of the window.

The phase plane trajectory is toggled by the [Show trajectory] button, and a line connecting the two graphs is toggled by the [Relate diagrams] button.

A slider at upper left controls a zoom feature in x and ẋ: the maximal displayed values of x and ẋ are read out to the right of the slider.

The [+ explanation] key opens a floating panel that computes and interprets every displayed quantity for the current parameter values. It floats above the app without changing the layout, can be dragged by its title bar, resized from its lower right corner, minimized, closed, or sent to a separate live tab.

Re-implementation in HTML/JavaScript of the MIT Mathlet "Forced Damped Vibrations" (original © 2008 H. Hohn, H. Miller and J.-M. Claus).
